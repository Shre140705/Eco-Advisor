## Interpretation

Glass dominates this particular profile because the current normalized data gives it high barrier, compatibility, reuse, and recovery indicators. That is a result of the chosen assumptions, not evidence that glass is universally preferable: mass, transport distance, breakage, return logistics, refill cycles, and local system performance are not fully modelled. The appropriate research conclusion is therefore that the case study demonstrates the workflow and exposes a trade-off profile; it does not establish a material recommendation for an operational supply chain.
