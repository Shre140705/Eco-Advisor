# Current limitations

1. Food and packaging profiles are curated ordinal assumptions for an educational decision-support release.
2. The synthetic Random Forest training set demonstrates a pipeline; it is not measured experimental evidence.
3. Packaging properties depend on grade, thickness, coating, format, formulation, and test conditions.
4. Recycling, composting, and recovery are region- and facility-dependent.
5. The Sustainability Score is not a certified LCA and the carbon indicator is comparative rather than absolute CO2e.
6. Food-contact status in the catalogue is not a legal approval.
7. Shelf-life results require laboratory or field validation before operational use.
