# EcoAdvisor design brief

## Direction

EcoAdvisor uses a calm, research-led sustainability dashboard direction: warm off-white canvas, deep forest green for trusted actions, moss and sea-glass accents for environmental signals, and graphite text for clarity. The interface should feel like a laboratory notebook translated into a modern product rather than a generic green marketing page.

## Principles

- Lead with the decision and supporting evidence.
- Separate preservation performance from sustainability claims.
- Make uncertainty and provenance visible without overwhelming the user.
- Use compact cards, clear tables, and a single strong call to action.
- Prefer data density with generous spacing over decorative illustration.

## Layout

A responsive two-column dashboard is used on desktop: a sticky input rail and a broad results workspace. On mobile, the input rail becomes a stacked form above results. Cards use thin borders, restrained shadows, and rounded corners; charts sit on quiet surfaces so the scores remain legible.

## Typography and voice

Use Inter/system sans for interface text, with compact uppercase labels for data-status chips. The voice is direct, transparent, and academically careful: “comparative indicator,” “model estimate,” and “requires local verification.”

## Signature identity

The project mark is a simple leaf wrapped around a circular decision node, representing circularity plus decision support. The wordmark is “EcoAdvisor.” The signature brand color is forest green `#1f6b4f`, supported by moss `#b9d7b0`, sand `#f4efe6`, ink `#18312b`, and amber `#d18b38` for caveats.

## Interaction

Every recommendation is actionable: the user sees the top package, alternatives, the reason it won, and the data caveat. Sliders update priority labels immediately. Animations are limited to chart/card entrance and score bars; no visual effect should imply scientific certainty.
